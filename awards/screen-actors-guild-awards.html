Awards & Critical Recognition

Reese Witherspoon Screen Actors Guild (SAG) Awards Record

Reese Witherspoon won the Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Leading Role in 2006 for Walk the Line, recognized by her actor peers worldwide. Witherspoon has garnered seven SAG Award nominations across lead actress and ensemble categories for films and series including Big Little Lies and The Morning Show. Her SAG honors highlight broad peer respect spanning three decades.

Historical Nominations & Wins

Year Category Project Outcome Context
2006 Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Leading Role Walk the Line 🏆 WINNER Peer-voted acting achievement award.
2015 Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Leading Role Wild ⭐ NOMINEE Lead actress performance honoring Cheryl Strayed's story.
2018 Outstanding Ensemble in a Drama Series Big Little Lies ⭐ NOMINEE Alongside Nicole Kidman, Laura Dern, and Shailene Woodley.
2020 & 2022 Outstanding Ensemble in a Drama Series The Morning Show ⭐ NOMINEE Alongside Jennifer Aniston, Steve Carell, and Billy Crudup.

About Reese's Awards Legacy

Beyond individual accolades, Reese's dual achievements as an Oscar-winning lead performer and Emmy-winning executive producer place her in an elite echelon of modern Hollywood creators who both command the screen and finance groundbreaking female-driven cinema.
