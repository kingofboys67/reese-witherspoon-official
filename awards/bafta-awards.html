Awards & Critical Recognition

Reese Witherspoon BAFTA Awards Record

Reese Witherspoon won the prestigious BAFTA Award for Best Actress in a Leading Role in February 2006 for her performance in Walk the Line at the British Academy Film Awards in London. She earned her second BAFTA nomination for Best Leading Actress in 2015 for Wild. The BAFTA honors represent international acclaim from the British film industry for Witherspoon's nuanced character work.
