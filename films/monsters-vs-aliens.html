Monsters vs. Aliens (2009)

Monsters vs. Aliens is a 2009 feature film starring Academy Award winner Reese Witherspoon as Susan Murphy / Ginormica (voice), directed by Rob Letterman & Conrad Vernon and produced by Lisa Stewart. Released theatrically by DreamWorks Animation, the project earned global recognition with a worldwide box office of $381M. Witherspoon's celebrated performance as Susan Murphy / Ginormica (voice) remains a standout touchstone in modern American cinema.

Synopsis & Cultural Impact

Reese voices Susan Murphy — an ordinary woman who transforms into a 49-foot giant after being struck by a meteorite on her wedding day and is recruited by the government to fight alien invaders. An animated smash grossing $381M worldwide, Reese's voice brings warmth and humour to a beloved character.
