Water for Elephants (2011)

Water for Elephants is a 2011 feature film starring Academy Award winner Reese Witherspoon as Marlena, directed by Francis Lawrence and produced by Gil Netter, Andrew R. Tennenbaum. Released theatrically by 20th Century Fox, the project earned global recognition with a worldwide box office of $117M. Witherspoon's celebrated performance as Marlena remains a standout touchstone in modern American cinema.

Synopsis & Cultural Impact

A Depression-era love story set inside the most dangerous place on earth — a traveling circus. Starring alongside Robert Pattinson and Christoph Waltz, adapted from Sara Gruen's beloved bestselling novel. Reese brings grace and vulnerability to the role of the star performer trapped in a dangerous marriage.
