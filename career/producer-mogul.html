Career Period · 2016–Present

Reese Witherspoon — Media Mogul & Hello Sunshine Era (2016–Present)

Beginning in 2016, Reese Witherspoon revolutionized the media landscape by founding Hello Sunshine, a multi-platform company dedicated to centering women in storytelling. Her leadership generated culture-defining television hits including HBO's Big Little Lies (winning the Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Limited Series in 2017) and Apple TV+'s flagship drama The Morning Show. Paired with Reese's Book Club—which has amplified over 100 female authors worldwide—Witherspoon emerged as one of the entertainment industry's most influential executives and visionary producers.

Key Highlights & Landmark Releases

Founded Hello Sunshine (2016) Role: Founder & Producer

Multi-platform media company valued at $900M upon Blackstone partnership.

Reese's Book Club Founded (2017) Role: Curator & Founder

Over 100 book selections; numerous adapted into award-winning screen productions.

Big Little Lies (HBO) (2017–2019) Role: Madeline / Executive Producer

Won 8 Primetime Emmy Awards and 4 Golden Globes.

The Morning Show (Apple TV+) (2019–Present) Role: Bradley Jackson / Executive Producer

Flagship streaming hit earning multiple Emmy, SAG, and Golden Globe nods.

Little Fires Everywhere (Hulu) (2020) Role: Elena Richardson / Executive Producer

Emmy-nominated adaptation of Celeste Ng's bestseller.

Enduring Impact of This Era

This period was pivotal in defining Reese’s multi-faceted creative identity. From daring creative risks to unprecedented commercial triumphs, the milestones achieved during this era continue to inspire artists and audiences worldwide.
