📚 Reese's Book Club

Stories That Shape Cultures

Launched in 2017 and now with millions of members worldwide, Reese's Book Club has become the most powerful literary force in popular culture — changing what gets published, what gets read, and who gets heard.

📗 Book Club Pick

Where the Crawdads Sing

Delia Owens' debut novel became a global phenomenon after Reese chose it. Sold 12+ million copies and was adapted into a 2022 film produced by Hello Sunshine.
